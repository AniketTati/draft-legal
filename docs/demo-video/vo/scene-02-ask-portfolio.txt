Start with the thing most contract tools cannot do. Ask your whole portfolio a question. A vendor has quoted two hundred thousand dollars for an eight week statement of work. Is that fair? The assistant searches every contract you have actually signed, and answers from your own history. It is reading your own paper, not a general model guessing at what the market charges. And it shows you how it got there. Open the tool call, and you can see exactly what was searched and what came back.
